# Privacy policy (sample)

Effective date: October 27, 2026. This sample is for the Aria demo ecommerce site and must be adapted to the laws and actual practices of the production business.

## Information collected

When you browse, register, sign in, or order, the site may process account username and password hash, contact details, saved addresses, order and shipping details, payment status and limited payment-method details, support messages, and basic technical logs. Guest checkout uses the contact and delivery details needed to fulfill and support the order. The demo database stores a payment-provider token and may store card brand and last four digits; it is not designed to store full card numbers.

## Purposes and sharing

Information is used to operate accounts, process payments, fulfill orders, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal and accounting obligations. Necessary information may be shared with payment processors, carriers, hosting providers, and professional advisers under appropriate arrangements. We do not sell personal information in this sample policy.

## Access, security, and retention

Access should be limited to staff who need information for their duties. The business should use appropriate technical and organizational safeguards and retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above or legal obligations. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices and contact

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or information about how your data is used. Contact customer support through the site to make a privacy request. The business should verify identity before disclosing or changing personal information and respond within legally required timelines.

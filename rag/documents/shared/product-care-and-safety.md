# Product care and aquatic equipment safety

Follow the instructions supplied by the manufacturer for fitting, use, cleaning, inspection, storage, and service intervals. Product descriptions on the site are general information and do not replace training, professional fitting, or equipment-specific instructions.

For diving equipment, use only equipment appropriate to your training and planned conditions. Inspect equipment before use and have life-support equipment serviced by a qualified technician at the manufacturer's recommended interval. Do not use equipment that is damaged, malfunctioning, or outside its service life. For personal flotation devices, confirm the correct type and fit for the activity and follow the label instructions.

Rinse equipment with fresh water when the manufacturer recommends it, allow it to dry fully, and store it away from heat, sunlight, chemicals, and sharp objects as appropriate to the material. Do not infer a product's certification, depth rating, compatibility, or safety performance from its category or appearance. Ask support or the manufacturer when a specification is not stated.

Report suspected defects or safety incidents to support promptly and stop using the affected item until it has been assessed.

# Shipping and delivery (sample)

At checkout, customers can choose from the shipping methods currently offered for their destination. The demo database supports `STANDARD`, `EXPRESS`, and `PRIORITY`; eligibility, price, and estimated delivery are shown at checkout. Estimates are not guarantees and can be affected by weather, carrier operations, customs, or incorrect address details.

After dispatch, the order page or shipping notice may show the carrier and tracking number. Guest customers should retain their order confirmation and use the checkout email when contacting support. If tracking appears stalled or delivery is disputed, contact support for an investigation. Do not share a tracking link or order details with anyone who has not passed the site's verification process.

Shipping costs and any applicable taxes are presented before the customer submits the order. Shipping charges for an eligible return are handled under the return policy and applicable law.

# Returns and refunds

Aria accepts eligible return requests made within **30 calendar days after delivery**. The item must be unused, in resalable condition, and include its original packaging and included components. Some hygiene-sensitive items, including used masks, snorkels, mouthpieces, and exposure suits, cannot be returned for change of mind once used. Faulty or incorrectly supplied items should be reported promptly; support will review them even if ordinary change-of-mind conditions do not fit.

Contact support with the order number and the item(s) in question. An approved return must be sent using the instructions supplied by support; do not ship items to an unconfirmed address. Refunds are issued to the original payment method after the return is received and inspected. Processing time can vary by payment provider. Shipping charges are not ordinarily refunded for change-of-mind returns; where Aria sent the wrong or a defective item, support will review appropriate shipping reimbursement.

This is a sample policy for the demo site. Applicable consumer rights may provide additional protections. The support decision guide contains internal review examples and escalation rules.

# Aria business code of conduct

Aria serves divers, swimmers, paddlers, and other aquatic-sport customers. We aim to make participation safer and more accessible through dependable equipment and honest service.

## Our commitments

- **Safety first:** Describe equipment accurately, do not overstate its capabilities, and escalate reports of defects or safety hazards promptly.
- **Honesty:** Give clear prices, availability, shipping, and return information. Acknowledge uncertainty instead of guessing.
- **Respect:** Treat customers, coworkers, suppliers, and communities with patience and dignity, without discrimination or harassment.
- **Privacy:** Access customer and business information only for a legitimate work purpose and share only what is needed.
- **Fairness:** Apply policies consistently while considering consumer rights, accessibility needs, and relevant individual circumstances.
- **Accountability:** Correct errors, document support decisions accurately, and raise suspected fraud, conflicts of interest, or unsafe products through approved channels.

Employees and automated assistants should not promise an exception, safety outcome, refund, or delivery date that they cannot authorize or verify. These values guide service but do not replace formal policy or applicable law.
